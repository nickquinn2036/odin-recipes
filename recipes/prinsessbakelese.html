PRINSESSBAKELESE RECIPE


A prinsessbakelse is a single-serving mini version of the traditional Swedish Prinsesstårta (Princess Cake). It consists of alternating layers of light, airy sponge cake, tart raspberry jam, and smooth vanilla pastry cream, topped with a high dome of stiffly whipped cream. The entire pastry is wrapped tightly in a thin layer of pale-green marzipan and traditionally garnished with a single pink marzipan rose.

INGREDIENTS

  • 4 large eggs
  • 1 cup granulated sugar
  • ½ cup all-purpose flour
  • ½ cup cornstarch
  • 1 tsp baking powder
  • 1 ⅔ cups whole milk
  • 3 large egg yolks
  • 1 tsp vanilla bean paste
  • 2 tbsp unsalted butter
  • ½ cup raspberry jam
  • 2 cups cold heavy whipping cream
  • 250g green marzipan
  • Small piece of pink marzipan (for the rose decoration)
  • Powdered sugar (for dusting)

STEPS TO FOLLOW

  1. Bake the sponge: Beat the eggs and sugar until pale and thick; fold in the flour, cornstarch, and baking powder, then bake in a sheet pan at 350°F (175°C) until golden, and let cool.
  2. Make the pastry cream: Whisk milk, egg yolks, cornstarch, sugar, and vanilla over medium heat until thick, stir in the butter, and cool completely in the fridge.
  3. Prepare the whipped cream: Whip the cold heavy cream to stiff peaks.
  4. Cut the cake: Use a round cookie cutter to stamp out small, individual cake circles from the sheet sponge.
  5. Layer the bases: Place the bottom cake circles down, spread a layer of raspberry jam, and top with a layer of the cooled pastry cream.
  6. Build the dome: Place a second cake circle on top, then pile on a generous dollop of whipped cream, shaping it into a smooth dome.
  7. Wrap with marzipan: Roll out the green marzipan thinly, drape it gently over the whipped cream dome, smooth the sides without creases, and trim the bottom edges neatly.
  8. Garnish and chill: Top with a small handmade pink marzipan rose, dust lightly with powdered sugar, and chill before serving.
