STRAWBERRY CAKE RECIPE


A strawberry cake is a vibrant, tender dessert featuring layers of moist cake infused with a highly concentrated strawberry puree reduction to give it a natural pink hue and intense berry flavor. It is traditionally paired with a rich, velvety strawberry cream cheese frosting or buttercream and decorated with fresh sliced strawberries.


INGREDIENTS

  • 2 ½ cups cake flour
  • 2 tsp baking powder
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 1 cup unsalted butter (softened)
  • 1 ¾ cups granulated sugar
  • 5 large egg whites (room temperature)
  • 2 tsp pure vanilla extract
  • ½ cup whole milk
  • 2 cups fresh strawberries (pureed and reduced to ½ cup)
  • 8 oz cream cheese (softened)
  • 4 cups powdered sugar
  • Additional fresh strawberries (for decoration)

STEPS TO FOLLOW

  1. Reduce the puree: Simmer the pureed strawberries in a saucepan over low heat until the liquid reduces by half and thickens into a paste, then let it cool completely.
  2. Mix dry ingredients: Whisk the cake flour, baking powder, and salt together in a bowl.
  3. Cream butter and sugar: Beat the softened butter and granulated sugar together on high speed until light and fluffy.
  4. Incorporate wet ingredients: Add the egg whites one by one, followed by the vanilla extract and the cooled strawberry reduction paste.
  5. Combine the batter: Alternately add the dry ingredients and the milk into the wet mixture, stirring gently just until combined.
  6. Bake the layers: Divide the batter into two greased 9-inch cake pans and bake at 350°F (175°C) for 30–35 minutes until a toothpick comes out clean, then let cool completely.
  7. Make the frosting: Beat the cream cheese and remaining butter until smooth, then gradually blend in the powdered sugar and a tablespoon of strawberry reduction until fluffy.
  8. Assemble the cake: Frost the top of the first cake layer, stack the second layer on top, cover the entire outside with the remaining frosting, and decorate with fresh strawberries.
