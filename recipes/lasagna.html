LASAGNA RECIPE


Lasagna is a classic Italian baked dish made of stacked layers of flat, wide pasta sheets alternating with various fillings. The traditional variation, Lasagne alla Bolognese, originates from the Emilia-Romagna region of Italy and features layers of rich meat ragù, creamy Béchamel sauce, and grated Parmigiano-Reggiano cheese.


Modern and regional adaptations are highly versatile, often incorporating ingredients like ricotta cheese, mozzarella, tomato marinara, spinach, mushrooms, or minced vegetables.To prepare the dish, the ingredients are assembled in a deep baking dish and cooked in an oven until the pasta is tender and the top layer turns golden and bubbly. The word "lasagna" refers to both the individual wide pasta ribbons and the completed casserole itself. It is celebrated worldwide as a hearty, comforting comfort food that is ideal for family gatherings and meal prepping due to how well it reheats.


Lasagne alla Bolognese

INGREDIENTS

Pasta & Sauces

  • Lasagna sheets: Wide, flat pasta noodles (either fresh or dried).
  • Meat ragù: A slow-cooked sauce made of minced beef, pork, pancetta, onions, carrots, celery, tomato paste, and white wine.
  • Béchamel sauce: A smooth white sauce made from a roux of butter and flour, cooked with milk and a pinch of nutmeg.

Cheeses & Toppings

  • Parmigiano-Reggiano: Finely grated and layered throughout for a rich, savory depth.

STEPS TO FOLLOW

  1. Boil the noodles: Bring a large pot of salted water to a boil, cook 12 lasagna noodles until al dente, drain, and rinse with cold water.
  2. Make the meat sauce: Cook 1 pound of ground beef, sweet Italian sausage, chopped onion, and minced garlic in a large skillet until brown, then drain the fat.
  3. Stir in pasta sauce and tomato paste, and simmer for 5 minutes.
  4. Mix the cheese: Combine 1.5 cups of mozzarella cheese, ¼ cup of Parmesan cheese, ricotta cheese, fresh parsley, and one egg in a medium bowl.
  5. Layer the dish: Spread 1 cup of meat sauce in a 9x13-inch baking pan, top with 3 lasagna noodles, add a layer of the ricotta cheese mixture, and more meat sauce.
  6. Add final toppings: Finish with a final layer of noodles topped with the remaining sauce and extra mozzarella and Parmesan cheese.
  7. Bake the lasagna: Cover the dish with foil and bake at 350°F for 45 minutes.
  8. Remove the foil and bake uncovered for another 15 minutes until golden and bubbly.
  9. Rest and serve: Let the lasagna rest for at least 15 minutes before cutting and serving.
